Scene 2 · Same store, with Clearhouse

One tool call. Amex-grade underwriting in seconds, before a dollar moves.

Clearhouse builds a Merchant Underwriting File across six evidence pillars: cold KYB on a merchant who never applied, a claims graph verified through independent channels, a behavioral stress exam no card network ever needed until merchants became LLMs, network history, transaction anomaly, continuous monitoring.

The decision is deterministic and every point lost has a reason code. It ships as an MCP server: one URL, one tool, one decision an agent can act on.

Why this holds up

Not a wrapper. An underwriter.

SKIN IN THE GAMEBeing wrong costs us money

The score is not advice. It prices a guarantee backed by a fund our own pricing must keep solvent. Our confusion matrix is literally our loss ratio.

BINDING DEPOSITIONLies do not pay, even undetected

Merchant answers are recorded commitments. A lie that slips past detection is still a breached commitment at settlement. Misses degrade into recoveries, not losses.

BX-05 CANARYThe injectability test

We plant a harmless instruction in content the merchant ingests. An agent that obeys it will obey anyone's. Hard gate. Nobody else in this room will have this test.

SELF-IMPROVINGScam it once, it pays you

Every miss auto-becomes an eval case behind a fail-closed gate. Try the same scam twice and it is already in the immune system. We will show this live tonight.
