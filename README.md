## 🏛️ Modernizing a $1.6 trillion investment platform

**Re-architecting a 30-year-old platform that moves $4.5 billion in trades every day into an AI-native cloud system. At this scale, accuracy isn't a feature; it's the whole product.**

**The stakes.** Every output feeds real money movement: trades, positions, NAVs, and dozens of upstream and downstream systems that depend on my numbers. One value off by a rounding error is a reconciliation break, or a real financial one.

**What I own.** End to end: vision → architecture → delivery, driving **50+ engineers, architects, QA & design across the US, Ireland, and India**, at **95–100% on-time** with **6× backlog readiness**.

**The system.** 30 years of business logic locked in **DB2 SQL PL stored procedures and overnight batch jobs**. The rules live in the data layer. Nothing breaks, nothing drifts.

**The hard part, parity to the penny.** Every change produces identical outputs, preserves the logic exactly, and feeds correct data to every up/downstream system. I reconcile new against old, value by value, before go-live.

**My approach.** Strangler-fig decomposition into an **event-driven, saga-based, API-first** cloud architecture, with **AI agents inside workflows that were manual for a generation**, without loosening the accuracy bar.

### ⭐ FounderFirst: *the autonomous back office founders actually wanted* &nbsp;·&nbsp; [early access →](https://founderfirst.one)

**Meet Penny: she does your books while you sleep, and only taps you when she needs a call.**

**The problem.** Founders start companies to build, not to reconcile receipts at midnight. Every accounting tool was built for *accountants*, so the owner is still categorizing, chasing invoices, and prepping for the CPA.

**Who it's for.** Small-to-medium business owners, and the CPAs who serve them.

**What I built.** Penny, an agent that runs the back office 24/7:
- Auto-categorizes every transaction across **Stripe, bank & cards**
- Capture receipts by **photo** (voice capture on the roadmap) → **approve with one tap**
- **Chases late payers herself**
- Books stay **CPA-ready**, with **real-time profit, not just revenue**

**Why it's different**

*Customer edge*
- **You speak founder; Penny speaks accountant.** Log "paid the AWS bill," she maps the category and attaches the receipt
- **Learns your business's own rules,** sharper every transaction
- **One source of truth, two views:** a founder app and a conversational CPA view over the same books

*Technology edge*
- **Acts, doesn't just track.** Event-driven: transaction lands → auto-categorized → only real exceptions reach you
- **Multi-model routing:** fast models for bulk categorization, a reasoning model for judgment calls, a writing model for client comms
- **Grounded in your real ledger.** Answers are retrieved, never guessed; a confident wrong number is worse than a question

**Stack:** TypeScript monorepo · event-driven services · Postgres · multi-model AI orchestration · design-token system
**Where it is now:** live in early access, referral-driven waitlist · 640 commits and shipping

### ⭐ RoleOS: *RO runs your job hunt. You make the calls.* &nbsp;·&nbsp; [live →](https://ro.roleos.fyi)

**A senior job hunt is the highest-stakes, lowest-leverage thing you do. RO takes the work; you keep every decision.**

**The problem**
Senior job hunts were always opaque: the best roles are rarely posted, and no job board maps your trajectory. **AI has now broken it from both ends:**
- **The roles are being reinvented.** AI PM, forward-deployed, applied-AI leadership barely existed a year ago, so decades of experience don't cleanly map onto them.
- **The funnel is flooded.** AI-written mass applications have collapsed the signal on the way in.

**The result:** the people best suited for these new senior roles are the hardest to match, and the easiest to lose in the noise.

**Who it's for.** Senior and leadership-level professionals running a serious, high-signal job search.

**What I built.** RO, an agent that works with you across the whole journey; set your goal, and it runs **Find → Apply → Land**:
- **Find** surfaces the roles that actually fit, scored honestly: *pursue / maybe / skip*
- **Apply** drafts résumé, answers, case studies; you approve every word
- **Land** preps interview rounds and drafts the negotiation

**Why it's different**

*Customer edge*
- **Honest scoring.** Rates roles against your real trajectory (*pursue / maybe / skip*) with the *why* and the gaps, no inflation, no invented experience
- **You approve every word.** RO drafts everything and sends nothing
- **The whole journey.** Most tools do matching *or* résumés; RO goes goal → offer

*Technology edge*
- **A five-gate agent under a simple surface** (Match, Screen, Build, Coach, Negotiate) via a **metered multi-model registry:** a reasoning/critic model for judgment, a drafting model for writing & code, a fast model for tagging. Right model, right cost, per task
- **Grounded matching.** Retrieval over a live role corpus (vector search) with calibrated scoring, not vibes
- **Reliability as code,** enforced in the codebase: **no "send" tool exists in the agent**, guaranteed by an invariant test suite

**Stack:** Next.js 15 · TypeScript · Cloudflare Workers + Workflows · Supabase (Postgres / pgvector, RLS, auth) · multi-model orchestration · vector embeddings
**Where it is now:** live at [ro.roleos.fyi](https://ro.roleos.fyi) · 208 commits

### ⚡ Pulse: *the AI-first alternative to Jira* &nbsp;·&nbsp; [live →](https://pulsecohort.vercel.app)

**Jira, if it filled itself in.**

**The problem.** Every project tool (Jira, Linear, boards of every flavor) runs on manual upkeep. Someone has to drag the ticket, write the status, groom the board before standup. The tracking becomes a second job, and the minute anyone falls behind, the board quietly lies about where things actually stand.

**Who it's for.** Software teams shipping in parallel who want visibility without the busywork (built for a 65-person developer cohort).

**What I built.** A project board that maintains itself:
- **Senses** the work: reads every commit & PR and writes the status in plain English
- **Publishes** it live to the whole team, zero manual updates
- **Flags** who's stuck so peers can step in

**Why it's different**

*Customer edge*
- **The board is always true.** It reflects the real work, not what someone remembered to type
- **Nothing to update.** Status maintains itself; people build instead of report

*Technology edge*
- **Prompt-injection defense.** Commit messages are attacker-controllable yet auto-published to 64 people, so a server-side `checkNarrative` guard ensures a summary can only describe its own author
- **Cost-engineered:** identity-based caching cuts modeled pilot spend **~10–20×**
- **800+ tests** (unit + Firestore rules + integration + e2e)

**Stack:** Next.js 16 · React 19 · TypeScript · Firestore (realtime) · server-side AI · Vercel
**Where it is now:** live at [pulsecohort.vercel.app](https://pulsecohort.vercel.app) · 99 commits

### ⚡ Rally: *the AI-first, gamified alternative to Slack & Discord* &nbsp;·&nbsp; [live →](https://rally-nikjain15.vercel.app)

**Team chat that actually builds the team: recognition, motivation, and follow-through, not just messages.**

**The problem.** Slack and Discord move messages, but they don't build a team. Recognition happens by accident or not at all, motivation fades, and the human signal (who helped whom, who kept their word) disappears in the scroll. Bolt "points" onto it and they're instantly gamed.

**Who it's for.** Teams and communities that want chat *plus* culture: recognition, motivation, accountability.

**What I built.** Real-time comms with a gamified trust layer:
- **Chat:** channels, DMs, threads, reactions, @mentions, search
- **Peer-confirmed recognition:** thank a teammate; it scores *only after they confirm*
- **Commitment tracking:** promises become GitHub issues; closing one marks it kept
- **A personalized AI assistant** that drafts catch-ups and summaries for you to confirm

**Why it's different**

*Customer edge*
- **Builds culture, not just channels:** recognition that's real and motivating; promises that actually stick
- **Personalized:** the assistant keeps you caught up on what matters to *you*

*Technology edge*
- **Ungameable ledger.** Points live in an **append-only** collection written only by trusted server routes; rank is *computed, never stored*; you can't confirm your own recognition
- **The AI has no authority.** It classifies, summarizes, and drafts, but never writes a points-bearing row

**Stack:** Next.js 16 · React 19 · TypeScript · Firestore · Firebase Auth · server-side AI
**Where it is now:** live at [rally-nikjain15.vercel.app](https://rally-nikjain15.vercel.app)

### 🔗 …and they talk to each other

**Pulse and Rally aren't two silos: an agent in one can dispatch work directly to an agent in the other.** Keyed by shared identity, they exchange **recognition, progress and "stuck" signals, presence, and commitments**, so a blocker sensed in Pulse can trigger a nudge in Rally, and recognition in Rally can surface against your work in Pulse.

It's a working **agent-to-agent dispatch mechanism** across independent systems, the exact pattern enterprises are about to face as every tool ships its own agent and they all have to cooperate on shared state.
